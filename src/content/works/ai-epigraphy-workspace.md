## Overview

Created for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**, the **AI Epigraphy Workspace** addresses the critical bottleneck in cultural heritage preservation: transcribing, deciphering, and cataloging weathered stone and copper-plate inscriptions across historical Indian scripts (Brahmi, Grantha, and ancient Devanagari).

### Key Capabilities
- **Surface Relief Enhancement:** Neural image pre-processing that enhances eroded stone carvings using directional photometric shadows.
- **Multimodal Character Recognition:** Custom fine-tuned Vision-Language models to identify damaged character glyphs with confidence scores.
- **Epigrapher Annotation Tools:** Interactive canvas for historians to segment ligature glyphs, align phonetic transliterations, and export standardized TEI-XML datasets.

### Technical Stack
- **Vision Models:** PyTorch, OpenCV, ViT (Vision Transformer)
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL with PostGIS
- **Frontend:** React, HTML5 Canvas, Tailwind CSS
