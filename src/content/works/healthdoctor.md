## Overview

**HealthDoctor** is an AI-assisted preliminary symptom screening and clinical triage system that predicts multi-disease risk profiles using supervised diagnostic classification models.

### Key Capabilities
- **Multi-Factor Symptom Cross-Correlation:** Evaluates patient-reported symptoms, demographic baselines, and historical markers against validated epidemiological datasets.
- **Explainable Diagnostic Outputs:** Generates confidence intervals and risk factors explaining why specific clinical pathways are recommended.
- **Secure Patient Privacy:** Zero persistent PII storage with client-side tokenized payload encryption.

### Technical Stack
- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn, Random Forests, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost
- **API Server:** Python, Flask, Gunicorn
- **Client:** Responsive HTML5/CSS3 medical portal
