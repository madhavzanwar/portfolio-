## Overview

**MemoryVerse AI** is an intelligent digital identity platform that automatically ingests student certificates, resumes, and project documentation, extracts verified metadata, constructs a chronological career timeline, and answers natural-language queries via semantic embeddings.

### Key Capabilities
- **Automated Document Intelligence:** Extracts credentials, dates, issuers, and skills from PDFs and images using multimodal vision-language models.
- **Semantic Career Q&A:** Vector search pipeline powered by Google Gemini embeddings that lets recruiters and mentors query qualifications in plain English.
- **Verifiable Credential Ledger:** Cryptographically structured records ensuring portfolio claims are backed by validated certificates.

### Technical Stack
- **Foundation AI:** Google Gemini 1.5 Pro & Gemini Flash
- **Vector Storage:** ChromaDB with Cosine distance indexing
- **Deployment:** Vercel serverless functions with fast edge caching
