## Overview

A collaborative scientific research investigation conducted at **Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE)**, examining the efficacy of tailored carbon nanotubes (CNTs), zinc oxide (ZnO) nanocomposites, and magnetic graphene oxide nanoparticles in adsorbing industrial heavy metal ions and decomposing toxic synthetic dyes from contaminated wastewater streams.

### Research Scope & Methodology
- **Adsorption Isotherm Analysis:** Evaluated Langmuir and Freundlich adsorption models to quantify maximum adsorption capacities ($q_{max}$) for lead ($Pb^{2+}$), cadmium ($Cd^{2+}$), and methylene blue dye.
- **Photocatalytic Degradation:** Measured solar and UV photocatalytic breakdown rates of persistent organic pollutants, achieving over 92% degradation efficiency under ambient conditions.
- **Magnetic Recovery:** Explored magnetic core-shell nanostructures allowing rapid post-treatment magnetic separation and material reusability over 5 consecutive cycles.
