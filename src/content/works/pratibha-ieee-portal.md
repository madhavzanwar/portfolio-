## Overview

Designed as the official brand identity and portal design system for **Pratibha**, the flagship national awards platform hosted by the **IEEE India Council**, recognizing outstanding student chapters, engineering leaders, and breakthrough technical contributions across the country.

### Design Principles
- **Geometric Rigor:** A refined vector emblem fusing the classic IEEE diamond geometry with the dynamic radiance of a torch and lotus blossom, symbolizing academic enlightenment.
- **Design Token Specification:** A standardized color, typography, and component hierarchy system providing consistent visual fidelity across digital certificates, mobile portals, and presentation collateral.
- **Accessibility & Contrast:** WCAG AAA-compliant color palettes balancing deep oceanic blues with warm ceremonial gold accents.

### Deliverables
- Vector Logo System (Primary, Monogram, Seal, Favicon)
- Design Token Library (Color Swatches, Spacing Grids, Typography Scales)
- Portal UI Wireframes & High-Fidelity Figma Prototypes
